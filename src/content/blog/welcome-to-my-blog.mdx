Welcome to my personal website! My name is Winston and I'm a software engineer based in the San Francisco Bay Area.

For the past few years, my personal website was written in pure HTML, CSS, and a minimal amount of JavaScript. I decided to switch to a more modern stack. The iteration of the website you are seeing today was built using Next.js, with all blog content written in MDX.

The current iteration of the website is a work in progress. I'm not particuarly happy with the nav layout and choice of font, and I'm planning on adding a contact form as well. If you have any suggestions or feedback, please feel free to reach out to me.
